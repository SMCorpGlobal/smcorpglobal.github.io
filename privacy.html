Privacy Policy — TwistHeros Uploader

Last updated: October 12, 2026

TwistHeros Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @TwistHeros to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uploads videos, captions and playlist entries to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It reads view statistics for the channel's own videos (YouTube Analytics) to improve future videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any other person's account or data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or track visitors.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. They can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.
